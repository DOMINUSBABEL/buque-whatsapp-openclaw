# ALARICUS Multi-Archetype Design System Architecture

## Overview
ALARICUS v2.4.0 introduces modular `design.md` specifications tailored to business domains and commercial archetypes.

### Archetypes:
1. **B2B Industrial & Automotive** (`design_systems/b2b_industrial_automotive.design.md`)
   - Palette: Industrial Gold (`#f59e0b`), Supercar Crimson (`#dc2626`), Deep Carbon (`#090d16`).
   - Focus: Technical reliability, diagnostic transparency, direct WhatsApp booking.
2. **B2C Gastronomy & Hospitality** (`design_systems/b2c_gastronomy_hospitality.design.md`)
   - Palette: Obsidian Flame (`#090d16`), Sourdough Crust (`#d97706`), Basil Leaf (`#10b981`).
   - Focus: Zero-commission direct WhatsApp orders, table reservations, artisanal showcase.
3. **B2C Wellness, Medical & Beauty** (`design_systems/b2c_wellness_medical_beauty.design.md`)
   - Palette: Arctic Clean Slate (`#06b6d4`), Velvet Rose (`#f43f5e`), Biosecurity Slate.
   - Focus: Clinical hygiene, personalized treatments, rapid appointment scheduling.
4. **B2B Tech, Software & Consulting** (`design_systems/b2b_tech_software_consulting.design.md`)
   - Palette: Cyber Neon Indigo (`#6366f1`), Sovereign Emerald (`#10b981`), Dark Terminal (`#020617`).
   - Focus: Quantifiable ROI calculators, data sovereignty, AI agent automation demo bookings.
5. **B2C Retail, Boutique & Fitness** (`design_systems/b2c_retail_boutique_fitness.design.md`)
   - Palette: Onyx Noir Gold Leaf (`#eab308`), Electric Lime (`#84cc16`).
   - Focus: Exclusive inventory, VIP shopping assistance, athletic training programs.
